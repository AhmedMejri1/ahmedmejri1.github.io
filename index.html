About

I'm an HPC Systems Engineer who diagnoses application, scheduler and storage issues, analyses job failures and performance bottlenecks, and keeps GPU clusters healthy. I administer PBS Professional and Slurm, and deploy enterprise-scale clusters with OpenHPC and Warewulf.

Today I run a GPU-based HPC platform for AI, machine learning and research workloads: Kubernetes resource management, NVIDIA MIG partitioning, BeeGFS storage, monitoring and capacity analysis, plus deploying and validating LLM inference with vLLM on NVIDIA H200. I document everything as SOPs and runbooks.
